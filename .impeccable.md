## Design Context

### Users

AAAD serves technically curious Android Auto users working on a phone, often with hundreds of
installed apps. They need to understand which apps already include a car experience, which apps
AAAD can make car-compatible, what a conversion will change, and whether a long-running operation
is progressing. During conversion they may be impatient or concerned about app data, installation
prompts, and whether it is safe to leave the screen.

### Brand Personality

Clear, capable, and system-native. Copy should be direct and specific without assuming knowledge
of Android package attribution, templates, or Shizuku internals.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use current Material 3 components and follow the device's light or dark system theme. Prefer a
calm, functional Android-native interface with strong state hierarchy over decorative styling.
Conversion state, queue position, progress, cancellation, and native car compatibility should be
recognisable without relying on color alone.

### Design Principles

1. Make current activity and the next available action obvious within two seconds.
2. Show complete descriptions; never hide consequential conversion details behind ellipses.
3. Treat long work as a queue with honest stage-based progress and a visible escape hatch.
4. Preserve originals by default and explain when Android will request installation confirmation.
5. Follow Material 3 system theming, accessibility, and 48dp touch targets across light and dark.
